Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us on this website.

Last updated October 25, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out a form, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, company, website and message. We use it to respond to your request and to talk with you about our services.

Where it goes

Form submissions are stored in HubSpot, the customer relationship management tool we use. We don't sell your information.

Calculators

Our free calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to us unless you choose to include the results with a form.

Analytics and cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand how people use this site. These tools may set cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email support@facebookadsagencychicago.com.
