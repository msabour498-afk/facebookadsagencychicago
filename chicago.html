Chicago, Illinois

Facebook ads in Chicago

Chicago is our home base, with an office at 229 W Madison St in the Loop, and the city is a market of very different neighborhoods rather than one audience.

Get a free audit Talk to us

Businesses here

A dense downtown full of offices and professional services sits next to residential neighborhoods with their own character and spending habits. That makes Chicago a strong market for law firms, real estate, med spas, fitness studios, restaurants and B2B companies, as long as targeting follows the neighborhoods you actually serve.

How we target Chicago

  • Neighborhood-level targeting by ZIP code or radius, such as the Loop, River North, West Loop, Lincoln Park, Lakeview, Logan Square and Hyde Park
  • Separate ads for downtown workers and for residents
  • Small radius targeting for studios, clinics and restaurants, since city customers rarely travel far
  • City-wide campaigns for services that come to the customer, such as HVAC and remodeling
