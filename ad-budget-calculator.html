How to read this

This is simple math on the numbers you enter: budget divided by cost per lead gives leads, and your close rate turns leads into customers. Real results move around month to month, and cost per lead changes with your offer, audience and creative.

If you don't know your cost per lead yet, try a few values to see a range. The free audit can give you a more grounded estimate for your market.
