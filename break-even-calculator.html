How to read this

Break-even ROAS is the revenue you need for every $1 of ad spend just to cover the cost of the ads. It's 1 divided by your gross margin.

Most you can pay per customer is your sale value times your margin. Most you can pay per lead takes your close rate into account. Use it to judge whether a campaign's cost per lead is healthy.

If customers buy again, use lifetime value instead of a single sale. Your break-even numbers will rise.
